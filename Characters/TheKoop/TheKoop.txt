The Koop is rude and grumpy and very strong. He does mostly punches and jumps rarely.  His special attack cooldown is twice as fast as others.

Special moves:
- Throws a server box at you, pulls out a giant server box and throws it at you.
- Crouches and pulls out a laptop to remove all your access to web services causing major momentary confusion of the enemy as they check their cell phone, watch, etc.
