The Koop is rude and grumpy. He does mostly punches and jumps rarely.

Health: 150
Strength: 150
Defense: 150

Special moves:
- Throws a server box at you, pulls out a giant server box and throws it at you.  Triggered with S, S, left click.
- Crouches and pulls out a laptop to remove all your access to web services causing major momentary confusion of the enemy as they check their cell phone, watch, etc.
