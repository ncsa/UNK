Story:
ChuckT, the university's most feared auditor, has declared the College of Engineering "fiscally
non-compliant." He has frozen every research budget, seized the coffee machines as unreported
assets, and barricaded himself inside Engineering Hall until every expense report since 1998 is
re-filed in triplicate. The player has a grant due at 5pm and needs one signature from inside.

Setting:
The walk across the main quad toward Engineering Hall, past old stone campus buildings. The audit
has spilled outside: stacks of file boxes, paper shredders and filing cabinets dragged out onto the
plaza, with red tape strung between the lamp posts.

Enemies:
- Audit Intern: a jittery intern in an oversized blazer and lanyard who swings a clipboard and throws stacks of forms; weak, but they come in groups (x5)
- Compliance Officer: a stern officer in a gray suit carrying a giant red DENIED stamp; blocks a lot and stamps hard (x3)
- Shredder Bot: a rolling office paper shredder on wheels that charges at your legs and spits confetti (x2)
- Expense Report Golem: a hulking figure made of crumpled receipts and binder clips; slow but hits hard, shows up near the end (x1)
