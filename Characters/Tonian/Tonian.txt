Tonian is arrogant, sassy, and mischievous.  He mixes punches and kicks, jumping occasionally.

Special moves:
- Giant denied stamp, he brings out an enormous DENIED stamp and stomps the other character with it, yelling out "DENIED!!".
- You are mean to me twirl, he spins with fists out causing multiple punches to hit nearby enemies, yelling out "why are you so mean to me!".
